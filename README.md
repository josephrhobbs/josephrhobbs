# Joseph R. Hobbs

Welcome to my GitHub profile, and thank you for visiting!

My name is Joseph Hobbs and I'm an engineer and independent researcher.  I did my bachelor's degree in aerospace engineering at MIT (class of 2025).  My academic interests are primarily focused on the intersection between applied mathematics and the practical discipline of engineering.  The last centuries have seen _incredible_ advances in mathematics: probability theory came to maturity in its contemporary form; the work of Shannon and others laid the foundations for information theory; we've come to see linear algebra, statistical and variational formulations of mechanics, and theoretical computer science with formalisms for groups, graphs, and geometry.

In recent decades, this growth has accelerated dramatically, and we've seen wild success in the ideas of dynamic programming, modern theories of optimal control, and convex optimization.  These threads of applied mathematics woven through the recent centuries of human development tie together the vast majority of my interests.
